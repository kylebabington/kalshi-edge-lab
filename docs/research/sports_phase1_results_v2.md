## 2. Reconciliation cohorts

* failed: independent-source series with >= 30 pre-test settled contracts and < 98% agreement: excluded from every cohort.
* test contracts whose independent payoff disagrees with Kalshi settlement are excluded from every cohort and counted per series; scored independent-source cohorts are settlement-concordant.
* sports_phase1_v1 admitted series with fewer than 30 pre-test contracts using all settled contracts (including the test period); v2 does not, so those series are exploratory.
